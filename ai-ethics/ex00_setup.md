## 2. Trusted sources on AI ethics

**OECD AI Principles** 
(https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles), credible because they were negotiated and adopted by dozens of national governments, giving them broad international legitimacy rather than reflecting one company's commercial interests.
**Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy / Usage Policy** 
(https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy), credible because it comes directly from a frontier AI lab explaining the concrete safety thresholds and constraints it holds itself to, so it shows practice rather than just theory.

## 3. What "responsible AI use" means to me

As someone building AI features rather than just using them, responsible AI use means designing the system so it fails safely and predictably - handling bad inputs, edge cases, and misuse gracefully instead of just working on the happy path. It also means being upfront with users about what the AI can and can't reliably do, so they don't place more trust in it than it deserves.
