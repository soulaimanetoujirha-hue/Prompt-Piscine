# Exercise 3: Image Prompt Design

## Prompt 1 (vague)
> a city at sunset

**Expected result:** Generic skyline silhouette, orange/pink sky, no strong style choice — could be any city, any art style, any angle. The model has to guess everything: real photo or painting? Modern or old? Ground-level or aerial?

## Prompt 2 (detailed)
> a futuristic city skyline at sunset, watercolour style, warm orange tones, wide aerial view

**Expected result:** A specific, cohesive image — soft watercolour brush textures instead of sharp photo-real edges, a bird's-eye perspective over the whole skyline, and a warm orange palette tying the whole scene together instead of a random mix of colors. Futuristic details (unusual building shapes, maybe flying vehicles or glass towers) would show up because the style was named directly.

*(No image tool was available in this environment, so both results are described rather than generated — but the difference in specificity is the same either way.)*

## One sentence
Adding style, palette, and camera angle turned a "guess what I mean" prompt into a directed one — the detail is what actually controls the output, not just the subject.
