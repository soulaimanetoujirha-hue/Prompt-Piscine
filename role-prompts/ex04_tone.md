## 1: invite a candidate to an interview on Friday at 3 PM

## 2: Frindly vs Professional
**Friendly version:**

Hi [Name],

Great news — we'd love to have you in for an interview! Are you free this Friday at 3 PM? Let us know if that works for you, and we'll send over the details.

Looking forward to meeting you!

Best,
[Your name]

---

**Professional version:**

Dear [Name],

We would like to invite you to an interview for the [position] role on Friday at 3 PM. Please confirm your availability at your earliest convenience, and we will follow up with further details.

Best regards,
[Your name]

## 3: confirm

Both versions state the same facts: an interview invitation, scheduled for Friday at 3 PM, with a request to confirm availability — only the tone (casual/enthusiastic vs. formal) differs.
